    TH(s) = (Kp + Ki/s)*EPS(s) + Kd*V(s)
    C(s)  = Kd*s + Kp + Ki/s = (0.000452 s^2 + 0.000595 s + 0.000397)/s  [rad/mm]
    Ceros del controlador: -0.659+0.667j  -0.659-0.667j   rad/s
  Ley discreta (cada cuadro, Ts = 0.0333 s), por eje:
    eps[k]    = x_hat[k] - r[k]                          [mm]
    u[k]      = Kp*eps[k] + I[k] + Kd*v_hat[k]              [rad]
    th_cmd[k] = min(max(u[k], -th_lim), th_lim),  th_lim = 10.0 deg
    nq[k]     = nq[k-1] + 1 si |v_hat[k]| < 25 mm/s; si no, 0
    I[k+1]    = I[k] + Ki*Ts*sat(eps[k], +-30 mm)  si nq[k] >= 4
                y no (th_cmd saturado y sign(eps) == sign(u))   (clamping)
                I[k+1] = I[k] en otro caso
